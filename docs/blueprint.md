# **App Name**: Quantum Nest

## Core Features:

- Earth Insights: Provides details about Earth's atmosphere, water, oxygen levels, and temperature.
- Planet Exploration: Allows users to explore a list of planets with details on their distance from the Sun, gravity, and atmosphere.
- Habitable Zone Explanation: Illustrates and explains the concept of the Goldilocks Zone with a diagram.
- NASA APOD Display: Fetches and displays NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day with its title, image, and explanation.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary background: Deep Space (#0B1026). Very dark blue to feel cosmic and modern.
- Secondary background: Cosmic Ocean (#123C69). Represents deep Earth oceans + space depth.
- Accent Primary: Life Green (#2ECC71). Symbolizes life, habitability, Earth.
- Accent Secondary: Astro Cyan (#00D4FF). Represents science, technology, exploration.
- Primary Text: Soft Stardust (#F5F7FA). Soft white, easy on eyes (better than pure white).
- Secondary Text: Nebula Gray (#AAB2D5). For subtitles and descriptions.
- Body font: 'Inter', sans-serif, to ensure the educational content remains easily legible.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk', sans-serif, providing a clean, modern look and suitable for informational apps.
- Simple and clear icons to represent different aspects of Earth and planets.
- Smooth transitions between screens to enhance user experience.
